# Technical implementation

The mathematical benchmark classifications use zero. In computation, a county is below its benchmark when its log departure is less than −ε, and aggregate attainment requires a mean departure at least −ε, with ε=10⁻¹². This is a floating-point equality convention, far smaller than the quotation grid. The aggregate alert uses the same less-than convention. Original decimal values determine quotation updates and constant runs.

For N counties and T attainment dates, observed and reference below-benchmark county–date totals are integers O and K_ref. Their means are O/(NT) and K_ref/(NT). Upper-tail ties use K_ref≥O exactly; paired signs use O>K_ref, with equality defining zero. The paired difference is (O−K_ref)/(NT). Draws receive equal weight even when T changes. An empty attainment set has no reference statistic; binary packets use UInt16 sentinel 65535 for missing reference counts. They are not resampled until nonempty.

All comparisons retain the original 108-date historical calendar and 207-date later calendar, including the three later incomplete dates. Aggregate analyses use the original 204-complete-date mask. Historical residual arrays have 108 positions, with donor support determined by each benchmark: the 2020 window uses positions 72–107. For that window, non-circular block lengths 3, 6 and 12 have 34, 31 and 25 possible starts. The month-specific benchmark matches month and 5/15/25 slot at each block start. Its historical blocks end within a year. Later paths use seasonal alignment; no last-to-first-year circular join is made.

The generator is PCG32-XSH-RR64/32, following its author’s reference implementation. Its Apache-2.0 license is in environment/LICENSE_PCG_APACHE_2.0.txt. Each benchmark and block length has independent outer, inner and fixed-reference streams. Groups share sampled dates within that configuration. State and stream selectors are stored as decimal strings; index packets contain the actual starts. Six selected configurations continue their 1,999-draw states to 9,999 draws. These precision checks do not replace the 1,999-draw main analysis. Stream definitions and states are in inputs/reference_experiments/T1_source and results/precision/*indices_metadata.json.

The fixed upper-tail estimate is (K+1)/(R+1), where K counts draws at least as large as the observation and R counts valid draws. Its Monte Carlo standard error is sqrt(Rq(1−q))/(R+1), q=K/R. The unadjusted positive fraction uses sqrt(q_pos(1−q_pos)/R). Exact binomial intervals describe Monte Carlo estimation uncertainty; quantiles use linear interpolation. These are separate from the paired percentile interval. Full-precision counts and results are supplied for both repetition counts.

National amplitude is max(P)/min(P)−1. Decimal endpoint strings are converted to scaled integers for strict comparisons with 10%, 20% and 30%. Missing national periods leave an unexceeded branch unresolved. An observed amplitude already above the threshold confirms an exceedance because further observations can only increase its range. A confirmed low-update branch also confirms the OR rule. Other products receive no substituted maize or urea national series.

The additional-product data distinguish source-local product identifiers, specification, unit and county identifier. The 610 response files include six HTTP failures. Capture is uneven: second pages occur on only three dates. The product registry and original responses document matching, duplicate reconciliation and the exact fields used in the 80,955-row panel.

Supplement tables use full-precision scientific values. The main article rounds effects and intervals to two decimals and ordinary tail estimates to three decimals; boundary reports and exact zero classifications remain identified. English display labels are generated from inputs/presentation_dictionary.json. The generated position map reverses the presentation source exactly to reports/supplement.md; original Chinese labels remain in the data and scientific source.
