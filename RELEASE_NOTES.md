# Agricultural price monitoring project

AgriPrice Monitor brings together the article, short project report, data, analysis programs, independent figures and supplementary results for county maize–urea price monitoring in Liaoning. The complete package includes all scientific inputs and reference outputs needed for offline reproduction.

Sixty deletion comparisons yield 53 strictly positive paired intervals and seven intervals containing zero. The five historically flagged counties have lower pooled maize and urea update rates than the comparison group in both later periods. All twenty long unchanged runs coincide with at least one updating peer; three coincide with peer-median amplitude above 20%. Full results appear in Supplement S11 and its twelve machine-readable result files.

The article and short report state the economic relationships and monitoring rules directly. The editorial update leaves all analytical inputs, reference results, figures and statistical values unchanged.

Supplement S10 covers month factors, quotation-update timing and external-reference checks. The unified reproduction entry includes the S11 calculations, result comparisons and editable report builds. A renamed Chinese font subset and its Open Font License are included for the project brief.
