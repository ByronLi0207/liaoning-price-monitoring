# Response to reviewers

Manuscript: *Benchmark choice and quotation persistence in county maize–urea price monitoring: Evidence from Liaoning, China*

We revised the manuscript’s language, organization and presentation to state its findings and methods more clearly. The responses below follow the numbering and comment summaries in the fourth-round editorial brief.

## Major comment 1 Contribution and main argument

> “贡献定位” — contribution and focus of the paper.

**Response.** We have reorganized the article around three findings. First, historical benchmark windows change aggregate attainment and monitoring alerts even when the observation calendar is held fixed. Second, persistent quotations enter the historical benchmark as well as subsequent index values. Third, the apparent excess in county incidence changes sharply when counties with persistent quotations are removed. The revised abstract states the attainment range of 34.31%–99.02% on 204 complete dates, the six-county aggregate-alert range of 0–131 dates, and the 54.64% shortfall contribution of Jianping and Qingyuan. The introductory contribution paragraph states the three contributions directly.

**Changes.** Abstract; final paragraph of Section 1; opening of Section 6.1; Section 8. The three contributions quantify benchmark effects on dates and episodes, describe quotation screening and its threshold grid, and connect the incidence comparisons to four disclosures in monitoring reports: reference years, county membership, quotation-update histories and alert thresholds.

## Major comment 2 Rationale for the historical benchmarks

> “基准依据” — basis for choosing the benchmark windows.

**Response.** We clarified the role of each historical window in Section 3.3. The 2018–2020 window covers three complete archive years after the maize procurement reform and before the 2021 fertilizer-price surge. The two-year windows omit each endpoint symmetrically; 2020 supplies the closest complete annual comparison; and the month-specific benchmark matches the calendar month. Section 5.2 reports annual geometric exchange ratios of 0.797, 0.800 and 0.997 for 2018, 2019 and 2020, respectively. Month indicators explain 4.97% of pooled historical log-ratio variation and 10.48% of the residual variation after county and year effects. The historical levels and month-factor results explain how window selection and seasonal matching enter the classifications.

**Changes.** Section 3.3; Section 5.2; Supplement S10.1 and Tables S10.1–S10.2.

## Major comment 3 Meaning of the paired distribution

> “配对区间的含义” — interpretation of the paired interval and its distinction from the fixed reference.

**Response.** We clarified the paired interval and its relation to the fixed-reference comparison in Section 4.2. Each paired draw re-estimates the historical benchmark, recalculates attainment dates and compares observed incidence with a historical-residual reference on those same dates. The percentile interval summarizes the observed-minus-reference contrast across benchmark re-estimation and residual-path draws. An interval entirely above zero identifies excess incidence that persists through this comparison; an interval including zero places the contrast within the range of historical variation.

We also explain the two comparisons together using the ten-county group under the 2019–2020 benchmark. The fixed reference holds the historical threshold and observed aggregate path fixed while varying county residuals; the paired comparison adds benchmark re-estimation. The fixed-reference tail estimate is 0.010, while the paired interval is [−0.97, 8.36] percentage points. This example shows how the interpretation changes when the estimated historical threshold enters the resampling comparison.

**Changes.** Section 4.2; Section 5.2 and Table 3; Section 6.1; revised Supplement S10.3 and S10.5.

## Major comment 5 Mechanisms behind persistent quotations

> “报价持续机制” — mechanisms behind repeated quotation values.

**Response.** We clarified the three observation mechanisms discussed in the paper: an unchanged local price, reuse of an earlier quotation, and a sampling point without new price information. The county histories identify different records for verification. Jianping has 35 maize updates and six urea updates in 311 valid opportunities; Qingyuan has 48 maize updates and eight urea updates in 313. Jianping’s unchanged 1.50-CNY urea quotation crosses the 2024 source-system change. Its six recorded urea updates have dispersed timing relative to confirmed national turns: three match within six months under the primary rule, falling to one with complete monthly coverage. The discussion connects these patterns to the records used for quotation verification: the sampling enterprise, observation date and price basis.

**Changes.** Section 1, paragraph 3; Section 5.3; Section 6.2; Supplement S10.2 and Tables S10.3–S10.5.

## Major comment 6 National price scope

> “全国价格口径” — scope of the national price comparison.

**Response.** We clarified the national series’ role as an external record of market movement during a constant county quotation run. The screen measures proportional changes within the national series over that run. With partial coverage, the observed maximum-to-minimum amplitude is a lower bound on the full amplitude of the same national window; an observed amplitude above the cutoff therefore confirms that the national series crossed the threshold.

Section 3.2 explains this within-series comparison, Section 4.4 states how coverage enters the screening decision, and Section 7 brings the differences in product specification and trading stage into the interpretation of the external signal.

**Changes.** Section 3.2; Section 4.4; Section 7.
